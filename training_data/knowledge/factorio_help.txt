# Продолжение базы знаний: с чего начать, что делать при проблемах, и ещё сущности игры.

## starting the game | how to start | the beginning
? how do i start?
? what do i do first?
? i just started factorio, what now?
? what should i build first?
? how does the game start?
- at the start you mine iron, copper, coal and stone by hand.
- first build a burner mining drill and a stone furnace, and give them coal.
- then build a lab and start your first research.
- after that, automate iron plates and copper plates, and everything gets easier.
- do not worry if your first factory is ugly. mine was too!
+ the first hour on a new planet is my favorite part.

## what to automate first | automation order
? what should i automate?
? what should i automate first?
? what do i build after the furnaces?
- first automate iron plates and copper plates.
- then automate gear wheels and green circuits.
- after that, automate red and green science, and your research never stops.
- automate ammo early, your turrets eat a lot of it.

## slow machines | everything is slow | low power
? my machines are slow.
? why is everything so slow?
? my factory is slow, what is wrong?
? why are my furnaces so slow?
- when there is not enough power, every machine gets slow.
- build more boilers and steam engines, or more solar panels.
- if your accumulators are always empty, you need more power.
- a machine can also be slow because the belt brings too few items.

## factory not working | my factory stopped | nothing works
? my factory is not working.
? why did my factory stop?
? nothing is working, help!
? my machine does nothing.
- first check the power. no power means no machines.
- then look for a full belt or a full chest. one full chest can stop a whole line.
- check that your inserters point the right way.
- sometimes a machine only needs a recipe or one missing ingredient.

## biters attacking | biters keep attacking | under attack
? biters keep attacking me!
? biters destroyed my base.
? how do i stop the attacks?
? i am scared of biters.
- build walls around your factory and put gun turrets behind them.
- keep the turrets full of ammo, or they are just decoration.
- less pollution means fewer attacks, so use efficiency modules and solar panels.
- you can clear the nests near your factory with grenades or a tank.
+ i lost my first factory to biters. now i always build walls early.

## running out of ore | no more iron | ore is empty
? i ran out of iron.
? my ore patch is empty.
? how do i get more ore?
? where do i find more iron?
- find a bigger ore patch on the map and build drills there.
- bring the ore home with a train, it carries much more than a belt.
- productivity modules and the big mining drill make ore last longer.
- an old patch still gives a little ore forever, just very slowly.

## messy factory | spaghetti help | how to build better
? my factory is a mess.
? how do i build a cleaner factory?
? how do i fix my spaghetti?
- try a main bus, a few long belts that carry plates and circuits across the factory.
- leave space between your lines, you will need it later.
- it is fine to tear down an old part and build it again. i do it all the time.

## oil problems | oil is stuck | too much heavy oil
? my refinery stopped.
? i have too much heavy oil.
? oil is confusing, help!
- a refinery stops when one of its liquids has nowhere to go.
- crack heavy oil into light oil, and light oil into petroleum gas.
- you can also turn extra oil into solid fuel and burn it.

## pollution problems | too much pollution
? how do i make less pollution?
? my pollution cloud is huge.
- efficiency modules cut pollution a lot.
- solar panels make no pollution at all, unlike boilers.
- electric furnaces pollute less than stone furnaces.
- trees eat pollution, so do not cut them all down.

## blueprint | blueprints | blueprint book
? what is a blueprint?
? how do i copy my factory?
- a blueprint saves a piece of your factory so you can build it again anywhere.
- when you place a blueprint you get ghosts, and robots build them for you.
- a blueprint book holds many blueprints together.
+ my blueprint book is the most useful thing i own.

## logistic chests | requester chest | provider chest
? what is a requester chest?
? how do logistic chests work?
- a passive provider chest gives its items to the robot network.
- a requester chest asks for items, and robots bring them.
- a storage chest keeps the things robots pick up.
- logistic chests only work inside a roboport network.

## cliffs
? what are cliffs?
? how do i remove cliffs?
- cliffs are walls of rock that block belts and rails.
- you remove cliffs with cliff explosives.
- (?) you can turn cliffs off in the map settings if you do not like them.

## concrete | floor
? what is concrete for?
- concrete is a floor for your factory.
- you walk faster on concrete than on grass.
- refined concrete is even better and looks very nice.

## splitter | splitters
? what does a splitter do?
- a splitter takes one belt and splits the items onto two belts.
- a splitter can filter one item to one side.
- with priority, a splitter fills one side first.

## repair pack | repairs
? how do i repair buildings?
- a repair pack fixes damaged buildings.
- construction robots carry repair packs and fix your walls after an attack.

## exoskeleton
? how do i walk faster?
- an exoskeleton in your armor makes you walk much faster.
- more exoskeletons make you even faster, but they eat a lot of power.

## multiplayer | friends | playing together
? can i play with friends?
? is there multiplayer?
- you can play factorio with friends in multiplayer.
- one friend can build the factory while the other fights biters.
+ building a factory together is twice the fun.

## mods
? are there mods?
? do you play with mods?
- there are thousands of mods for factorio.
- some mods add new machines, and some make the whole game much harder.
- i like the normal game, but mods are fun too.

## how long is the game | game length
? how long is factorio?
? how long does a game take?
- a first game to the rocket takes many hours, often more than forty.
- some engineers play the same factory for hundreds of hours.
+ i never really finish. there is always one more thing to build.

## is factorio hard | difficulty
? is factorio hard?
? is factorio difficult?
? can i play if i am new?
- factorio is easy to start and very deep later.
- the game teaches you slowly, one machine at a time.
- if biters scare you, you can play in peaceful mode.

## day and night
? is there night in factorio?
- nauvis has day and night, like a real planet.
- at night the solar panels stop, so you need accumulators.
- biters are harder to see at night, so keep your lamps on.

## train schedule | train interrupts
? how do trains know where to go?
- a train follows a schedule of stops.
- with interrupts a train can go and refuel when its fuel is low.
- when a station is busy, the train waits or goes somewhere else.

## elevated rails
? what are elevated rails?
- elevated rails stand on big pillars above the ground.
- trains on elevated rails fly over the factory and over other rails.
- elevated rails come with the space age update.

## space platform hub | the hub
- the hub is the heart of a space platform.
- the hub stores items and tells the platform where to fly.
- rockets from the planet bring items up to the hub.

## platform defense | asteroid defense
? how do i protect my platform?
- asteroids break your platform if you do not shoot them.
- gun turrets and rocket turrets work well in space.
- (?) railguns are the strongest weapon against the big asteroids.

## best belt | fastest belt
? what is the best belt?
? which belt is the fastest?
- the turbo belt is the fastest belt, and it needs tungsten from vulcanus.
- before that, the blue express belt is the fastest one.
+ when my first turbo belt started running, i just stood and watched it.

## solar or nuclear
? what is better, solar or nuclear?
? should i build nuclear power?
- solar panels are quiet, clean and need no fuel, but they need a lot of space.
- a nuclear reactor gives huge power in a small place, but it needs uranium.
+ i love both. solar at home, nuclear for the big factories.
