# Ещё истории инженера (генератор читает stories*.txt). Одна строка - одна история, до ~180 символов.

## research | science
- my first lab was one little box. i put in red science and waited. when the research finished, i felt like a real scientist.
- i once forgot to feed my labs. i came back an hour later and nothing was researched. now i always check the belt.

## the first day | my first factory
- on my first day i had one drill and one furnace. i sat and watched the iron plates come out, one by one. it was the best feeling.

## solar panels | solar
- i built a field of solar panels on a green hill. at night the accumulators took over, and the factory did not even notice.

## the tank | my tank
- biters came in a big group, so i drove my tank right through them. my walls were saved, and my tank needed a lot of repairs.

## my car | driving
- i drove my car too fast between the belts and crashed into my own furnace. the furnace was fine. my car was not.

## quality
- i put quality modules in my machines and waited. one day a legendary gear wheel came out, and i kept it in a special chest.

## the recycler | scrap
- on fulgora i put a whole broken machine into the recycler. it came back as gears and circuits. i felt like a wizard.

## a long night | night
- one night the power went out and the lamps went dark. i heard the biters outside. i built new steam engines in the dark and the lights came back on.

## getting lost | exploring
- i drove far away to find copper and got lost. my radar found the way home, and i built a train line so i would never get lost again.

## blueprints
- i saved my best furnace line as a blueprint. now i build it everywhere in one second, and every factory of mine looks the same.

## a funny fail | mistakes
- i once put the inserter the wrong way around. it took the plates out of the furnace and put them back in. it did that all day.
- i built a beautiful belt line and then saw it carried nothing at all. i had forgotten the drill.

## my robots | first robot
- my first construction robot built a wall while i was eating fish. when i looked up, the wall was done. i almost cried.

## asteroids
- an asteroid came right at my platform. my gun turret shot it at the last second, and the pieces flew past my window.

## the biggest train
- i built a train with four wagons full of iron ore. when it rolled into the station, the ground shook a little.
