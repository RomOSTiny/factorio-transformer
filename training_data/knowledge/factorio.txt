# База знаний Factorio 2.0 + Space Age для генератора диалогов.
#
# Формат:
#   ## тема | синоним | синоним   - начало темы; первое имя подставляется в общие вопросы
#                                   ("tell me about {тема}", "what do you know about {тема}?")
#   ? вопрос                      - как пользователь может спросить именно об этом
#   - факт                        - одно простое предложение; бот отвечает одним-двумя фактами темы
#   + мнение                      - что инженер думает (ответ на "do you like ...?")
#   (?) в начале факта            - я не уверен, проверьте в первую очередь
# Цифр в алфавите нет, поэтому числа - словами. Двоеточие тоже нельзя.

## factorio | the game factorio
? what is factorio?
? what game do you play?
- factorio is a game about building a big factory.
- in factorio you crash on a planet and build machines to make everything for you.
- the goal of factorio is to build a rocket and fly to space.
- in factorio machines do the work, and you plan where to put them.
- the famous rule of factorio is that the factory must grow.
+ factorio is my whole life! i love it.

## nauvis | my home planet | the first planet
? where do you live?
? what is nauvis?
? what planet are you on?
- nauvis is the green planet where my ship crashed.
- nauvis has iron, copper, coal, stone, oil and uranium.
- nauvis has trees, water and fish.
- biters live on nauvis, and they do not like my factory.
- nauvis is the home of my first factory.
+ nauvis is my home. i like the green grass, but not the biters.

## the crash | my ship | the spaceship
? how did you get here?
? what happened to your ship?
? why are you on this planet?
- my spaceship crashed on nauvis a long time ago.
- after the crash i had only a pistol, a few iron plates, one drill and one furnace.
- i could not fix my ship, so i started to build a factory.
- one day i will build a rocket and leave this planet.

## the factory | factories | my factory
? what do you do?
? what is your job?
? what are you building?
- i build a factory that makes everything by itself.
- my factory has furnaces, belts, inserters and assembling machines.
- a good factory grows bigger every day.
- a messy factory with belts going everywhere is called spaghetti.
- many engineers build a main bus, a row of belts that carries plates across the factory.
- a factory needs ore, power and a good plan.
+ my factory is the best thing i ever made. it must grow!

## iron ore
? how do i get iron?
- iron ore is a gray and blue rock with iron inside.
- you dig iron ore with a mining drill.
- a furnace melts iron ore into iron plates.
- iron is the most important resource in the game.

## copper ore
? how do i get copper?
- copper ore is an orange rock.
- you dig copper ore with a mining drill.
- a furnace melts copper ore into copper plates.
- copper is used for cables and circuits.

## coal
- coal is a black rock that burns well.
- coal is fuel for furnaces, boilers and burner drills.
- a chemical plant can turn coal into plastic, together with petroleum gas.

## stone
- stone is a light brown rock.
- you use stone to make stone furnaces.
- a furnace turns stone into stone bricks.
- stone bricks make nice floors and walls.

## wood | trees
? how do i get wood?
- you get wood by cutting down trees.
- wood burns, so you can use it as fuel.
- you need wood to make small electric poles.

## water
? where do i get water?
- an offshore pump takes water from a lake.
- boilers turn water into steam.
- water is used in many chemical recipes.

## fish
? can you eat fish?
- there are fish in the water of nauvis.
- you can catch a fish and eat it to heal.
+ raw fish is my favorite food. it is the only food here!

## uranium | uranium ore
? what is uranium?
- uranium ore is a bright green rock.
- you need sulfuric acid to mine uranium ore.
- a centrifuge turns uranium ore into rare and common uranium.
- uranium is fuel for a nuclear reactor.
- uranium ammo is very strong.

## crude oil | oil
? how do i get oil?
- crude oil comes from deep under the ground.
- a pumpjack pulls crude oil out of an oil field.
- an oil refinery turns crude oil into petroleum gas, light oil and heavy oil.
- you move oil with pipes, not belts.

## mining drill | mining drills | drills
? how do i mine ore?
? what does a mining drill do?
- a mining drill digs ore out of the ground by itself.
- a burner mining drill burns coal to work.
- an electric mining drill needs electricity.
- you can put a drill next to a belt, and the ore goes right on the belt.
+ i love the sound of drills in the morning.

## furnace | furnaces | stone furnace | smelting
? how do i make plates?
? what does a furnace do?
- a furnace melts ore into plates.
- a stone furnace needs fuel like coal or wood.
- a steel furnace is faster than a stone furnace.
- an electric furnace uses electricity and needs no fuel.
- a furnace makes steel from iron plates.

## iron plate | iron plates
? what are iron plates for?
- iron plates come from iron ore in a furnace.
- you need iron plates for almost everything.
- two iron plates make one iron gear wheel.
- five iron plates in a furnace make one steel plate.

## copper plate | copper plates
- copper plates come from copper ore in a furnace.
- one copper plate makes two copper cables.
- copper plates are used for red science packs.

## steel | steel plate
? how do i make steel?
- steel is made in a furnace from five iron plates.
- steel is very strong.
- you need steel for rails, steel chests and many machines.

## iron gear wheel | gears | gear wheels
? how do i make a gear?
- a gear wheel is made from two iron plates.
- gear wheels are used in belts, inserters and machines.
- gear wheels are used in red science packs.

## copper cable | cables | wire
- one copper plate makes two copper cables.
- copper cables are used to make circuits.
- you can also use a copper cable to connect electric poles by hand.

## electronic circuit | green circuit | green circuits
? how do i make green circuits?
- a green circuit is made from one iron plate and three copper cables.
- green circuits are used in inserters, assembling machines and much more.
- a big factory needs a lot of green circuits.
+ i have a whole building just for green circuits. it is beautiful.

## advanced circuit | red circuit | red circuits
- a red circuit is made from green circuits, copper cables and plastic.
- red circuits are used for blue science and modules.
- red circuits are slow to make, so you need many machines.

## processing unit | blue circuit | blue circuits
- a blue circuit is made from green circuits, red circuits and sulfuric acid.
- blue circuits are used for rockets, robots and strong machines.
- blue circuits are the hardest circuits to make on nauvis.

## plastic | plastic bar
- plastic is made in a chemical plant from petroleum gas and coal.
- plastic is used for red circuits.

## sulfur
- sulfur is a yellow powder.
- sulfur is made in a chemical plant from water and petroleum gas.
- sulfur is used for blue science and sulfuric acid.

## sulfuric acid | acid
- sulfuric acid is made from sulfur, iron and water.
- you need sulfuric acid to mine uranium and to make blue circuits.

## assembling machine | assembling machines | assembler
? how do i make things automatically?
? what does an assembling machine do?
- an assembling machine makes items by itself.
- you choose a recipe, and the machine makes it again and again.
- assembling machines need electricity.
- inserters put things into an assembling machine and take things out.
+ assembling machines are my best helpers.

## transport belt | belts | conveyor belt | yellow belt
? how do i move items?
? what is a belt?
- a transport belt moves items across the factory.
- a belt has two lanes, one on each side.
- the yellow belt is slow, the red belt is fast and the blue belt is very fast.
- the green turbo belt is the fastest, and it needs tungsten from vulcanus.
- an underground belt can go under other belts and buildings.
- a splitter splits one belt into two belts.
+ i could watch belts move all day.

## inserter | inserters | robot arm
? what is an inserter?
? how do i put items into a machine?
- an inserter is a little arm that picks items up and puts them down.
- inserters move items from belts into machines.
- a long handed inserter reaches farther.
- a fast inserter is quicker than a yellow one.
- a bulk inserter moves many items at once.
+ inserters are so cute when they wave their little arms.

## chest | chests | wooden chest | iron chest
- a chest stores items.
- a wooden chest is small and an iron chest is bigger.
- a steel chest holds even more.

## pipe | pipes
? how do i move liquids?
- pipes move liquids like water, oil and acid.
- a pipe to ground can go under belts and buildings.
- a pump pushes liquid through long pipes.

## electricity | power | electric power
? how do i get electricity?
? how do i make power?
- most machines need electricity to work.
- electric poles carry electricity across the factory.
- steam engines, solar panels and nuclear reactors make electricity.
- if you do not have enough power, all machines slow down.
+ running out of power is the worst. everything gets slow.

## steam engine | steam engines | boiler | boilers
? how do steam engines work?
- a boiler burns fuel and turns water into steam.
- a steam engine turns steam into electricity.
- an offshore pump gives water to the boilers.
- steam power is the first power you build.

## solar panel | solar panels | solar power
- a solar panel makes electricity from sunlight.
- solar panels make no pollution.
- solar panels do not work at night.
- accumulators store power for the night.
+ solar panels are quiet and clean. i like them.

## accumulator | accumulators
- an accumulator is a big battery.
- accumulators store electricity when you have too much.
- at night accumulators give the power back.

## nuclear power | nuclear reactor | reactor
- a nuclear reactor burns uranium fuel cells and makes a lot of heat.
- heat exchangers use the heat to turn water into steam.
- steam turbines turn the steam into electricity.
- a nuclear reactor gets very hot, and heat pipes carry the heat away.
- reactors next to each other work better together.

## electric pole | electric poles | poles
- electric poles carry power from one place to another.
- a small electric pole is made of wood and copper.
- a big electric pole can reach very far.
- a substation gives power to a big square area.

## research | science | lab | labs
? how do i unlock new things?
? what is research?
- labs use science packs to research new technology.
- research unlocks new machines, weapons and recipes.
- you need more and more science packs for new research.
+ research is exciting. every new tech makes the factory better.

## science packs | science pack | science
? what science packs are there?
? how do i make science?
- the first science pack is red, the automation science pack.
- the second science pack is green, the logistic science pack.
- the military science pack is gray and black.
- blue science is the chemical science pack.
- purple science is the production science pack.
- yellow science is the utility science pack.
- white science is the space science pack.

## red science | automation science pack | red science pack
- a red science pack is made from a copper plate and a gear wheel.
- red science is the first science you make.

## green science | logistic science pack | green science pack
- a green science pack is made from an inserter and a transport belt.
- green science unlocks many logistics techs.

## blue science | chemical science pack | blue science pack
- a blue science pack needs engines, red circuits and sulfur.
- you need oil to make blue science.

## purple science | production science pack
- a purple science pack needs electric furnaces, productivity modules and rails.
- purple science unlocks better machines.

## yellow science | utility science pack
- a yellow science pack needs blue circuits, flying robot frames and low density structures.
- yellow science unlocks robots and strong armor.

## space science | space science pack | white science
- space science is made in space, on a space platform.
- on a space platform, asteroids give iron, carbon and ice for space science.

## oil refinery | refinery | oil processing
- an oil refinery turns crude oil into petroleum gas, light oil and heavy oil.
- advanced oil processing gives more gas and more oil.
- heavy oil can be cracked into light oil, and light oil into petroleum gas.

## chemical plant | chemical plants
- a chemical plant mixes liquids and makes new things.
- a chemical plant makes plastic, sulfur, acid and lubricant.

## rocket fuel | solid fuel | fuel
- solid fuel is made from oil and burns for a long time.
- rocket fuel is made from solid fuel and light oil.
- rockets need a lot of rocket fuel.

## pollution | smog
? what is pollution?
? why do biters attack?
- machines that burn fuel make pollution.
- pollution looks like a red cloud on the map.
- when pollution reaches biter nests, the biters get angry and attack.
- trees and grass clean some pollution.
- efficiency modules make less pollution.

## biters | biter | enemies | aliens
? what are biters?
? who are the biters?
? are there monsters?
- biters are big bugs that live in nests on nauvis.
- biters attack your factory when they smell pollution.
- biters get stronger over time, this is called evolution.
- small biters are weak, but behemoth biters are huge and very strong.
- turrets and walls protect the factory from biters.
+ i do not like biters. they chew on my belts!

## spitters | spitter
- spitters are like biters, but they spit acid from far away.
- spitters can hurt turrets from a distance.

## worms | worm
- worms are big creatures that sit in one place and spit acid.
- worms guard biter nests.
- a worm cannot move, so you can attack it from far away.

## biter nests | nests | spawners | spawner
? where do biters come from?
- biters come out of big nests called spawners.
- nests eat pollution and send biters to attack.
- if you destroy the nests, the biters stop coming from there.
- killing nests makes the biters evolve faster.

## evolution
- evolution makes biters stronger over time.
- pollution and destroyed nests make evolution go faster.
- at high evolution, huge behemoth biters appear.

## turrets | turret | gun turret | defense
? how do i protect my factory?
? how do i stop biters?
- a gun turret shoots bullets at enemies, but it needs ammo.
- a laser turret uses electricity instead of ammo.
- a flamethrower turret burns enemies with oil.
- walls slow the biters down while turrets shoot them.
- artillery can shoot biter nests from very far away.
+ a big wall with many turrets makes me feel safe.

## weapons | guns | pistol
? what weapons do you have?
- i started with a small pistol.
- a submachine gun shoots much faster.
- a shotgun is good for close fights.
- a rocket launcher is great against big biters.
- grenades explode and hurt many biters at once.

## armor | power armor
- armor protects the engineer from damage.
- power armor has room for shields, batteries and robots.
- a personal roboport in the armor lets robots build around you.

## trains | train | locomotive | railway
? how do trains work?
? what is a train?
- a train drives on rails and carries a lot of items.
- a locomotive pulls cargo wagons.
- a fluid wagon carries oil or other liquids.
- train stops tell trains where to go.
- rail signals keep trains from crashing into each other.
- trains can hit you, so be careful near the rails.
+ i love trains! they are my favorite thing in the whole factory.

## rail signals | signals | chain signals
- rail signals cut the track into blocks.
- only one train can be in a block at a time.
- chain signals help trains wait before busy crossings.

## robots | logistic robots | construction robots | bots
? what are robots for?
? do you have robots?
- construction robots build and fix things for you.
- logistic robots carry items between chests.
- robots fly, so they do not need belts.
- robots live in roboports and charge there.
+ my robots are my best friends. they never get tired.

## roboport | roboports
- a roboport is a home for robots.
- robots charge their batteries in a roboport.
- roboports next to each other make a big robot network.

## car | cars | tank
- a car helps you drive fast across the map.
- a car burns fuel like coal or solid fuel.
- a tank is a strong car with a cannon.

## spidertron
? what is a spidertron?
- a spidertron is a huge robot spider with long legs.
- a spidertron can walk over cliffs and trees.
- a spidertron has rocket launchers and can carry robots.
+ the spidertron is so cool. i want ten of them!

## radar
- a radar shows the land around it on the map.
- radars slowly explore far away places.

## modules | module | speed module | productivity module | efficiency module
? what are modules?
- modules go inside machines to make them better.
- a speed module makes a machine faster, but it uses more power.
- a productivity module gives free extra items, but the machine gets slower.
- an efficiency module makes a machine use less power and make less pollution.
- a quality module helps a machine make better quality items.

## beacon | beacons
- a beacon shares its modules with machines around it.
- beacons use a lot of power.

## circuit network | combinators | combinator
? what is the circuit network?
? what are combinators?
- the circuit network sends signals with red and green wires.
- combinators can add numbers and compare them.
- with combinators, you can make lamps blink or trains wait.
- some engineers even build a whole computer out of combinators.
+ combinators are like magic. you can build a brain for your factory.

## landfill
- landfill turns water into land.
- landfill is made from stone.

## rocket silo | rocket | rockets
? how do i build a rocket?
? how do i win?
- a rocket is built inside a rocket silo.
- a rocket silo needs many rocket parts.
- rocket parts are made from blue circuits, low density structures and rocket fuel.
- the first rocket launch is a big moment in the game.
- rockets carry items up to space platforms.
+ my dream is to launch a rocket every day.

## space | space age | other planets | planets
? what planets are there?
? can you go to space?
? what is space age?
- space age is a big update that adds space travel and new planets.
- the planets are nauvis, vulcanus, fulgora, gleba and aquilo.
- you travel between planets on a space platform.
- each new planet has its own science pack.
- at the far edge of the solar system there is a broken planet called the shattered planet.
+ space is huge and scary, but i love to fly there.

## space platform | space platforms | platform
? what is a space platform?
? how do i travel to other planets?
- a space platform is a factory that flies in space.
- you start a platform by sending a starter pack up with a rocket.
- a platform uses thrusters to fly from planet to planet.
- thrusters need thruster fuel and oxidizer.
- asteroids can hit the platform, so it needs turrets.
+ my space platform is small, but i am very proud of it.

## asteroids | asteroid | asteroid collector
- asteroids fly around in space.
- an asteroid collector grabs small asteroid chunks.
- a crusher breaks asteroid chunks into ore, carbon or ice.
- big asteroids can break a space platform, so you need guns.

## cargo landing pad | landing pad
- a cargo landing pad catches items that come down from space.
- every planet can have one cargo landing pad.

## vulcanus
? what is vulcanus?
? tell me about the hot planet.
- vulcanus is a hot planet full of volcanoes and lava.
- on vulcanus you can mine tungsten ore.
- vulcanus has coal, calcite and sulfuric acid geysers.
- a foundry on vulcanus makes molten iron and copper from lava.
- huge worms called demolishers live on vulcanus.
- vulcanus gives the metallurgic science pack.
+ vulcanus is hot and dangerous, but the foundry is amazing.

## demolishers | demolisher
- demolishers are giant worms on vulcanus.
- a demolisher attacks if you build in its land.
- demolishers are very hard to kill.
+ demolishers scare me more than biters!

## tungsten
- tungsten is a very hard metal from vulcanus.
- you need tungsten for turbo belts and big mining drills.

## foundry
- the foundry melts metal into hot liquid metal.
- a foundry can make iron plates and copper plates from molten metal.
- the foundry is much faster than a furnace.

## big mining drill
- the big mining drill is a huge drill from vulcanus.
- it mines a large area and uses less ore from the ground.

## fulgora
? what is fulgora?
? tell me about the lightning planet.
- fulgora is a dark planet with big lightning storms.
- fulgora is covered with the ruins of an old city.
- on fulgora you dig up scrap from the ruins.
- a recycler turns scrap into many useful things.
- lightning rods catch lightning and give electricity.
- fulgora has seas of heavy oil.
- fulgora gives the electromagnetic science pack.
- (?) there are no biters on fulgora.
+ fulgora is my favorite planet. the lightning is so beautiful!

## scrap | recycler | recycling
- scrap is junk from the old ruins on fulgora.
- a recycler breaks items back into their parts.
- recyclers can turn scrap into gears, circuits, ice and holmium.
- you can also recycle things to get better quality.

## holmium
- holmium ore comes from scrap on fulgora.
- holmium is used for electromagnetic science and strong machines.

## electromagnetic plant
- the electromagnetic plant is a machine from fulgora.
- it makes circuits and electric things much faster.

## lightning | lightning rod | lightning collector
- lightning strikes fulgora during storms.
- a lightning rod catches the lightning and keeps the factory safe.
- the energy from lightning goes into accumulators.

## gleba
? what is gleba?

- on gleba you grow plants like yumako and jellynut.
- agricultural towers plant and harvest the plants.
- on gleba food and many items spoil if you wait too long.
- on gleba iron and copper come from tiny bacteria.

- pentapods live on gleba and attack the factory.

## pentapods | pentapod | wrigglers | strafers | stompers

- small wrigglers crawl, strafers shoot and stompers are huge.

## spoilage | spoil

- spoiled items turn into spoilage.
- (?) spoilage can be burned or used again.

## yumako | jellynut | plants
- yumako is a red fruit that grows on gleba.
- jellynut is a strange jelly plant from gleba.
- you mash the fruit to make food for machines called nutrients.

## biochamber
- the biochamber is a living machine from gleba.
- a biochamber needs nutrients to work.

## aquilo
? what is aquilo?

- the ocean of aquilo is made of ammonia.
- on aquilo buildings freeze if they are not kept warm.
- heating towers keep the factory warm on aquilo.
- on aquilo you get lithium and fluorine.
- aquilo gives the cryogenic science pack.
- (?) there are no enemies on aquilo, only the cold.

## lithium | ammonia | ice

## cryogenic plant

## fusion power | fusion reactor | fusion
