# Короткие истории инженера (ответ на "tell me a story about ...").
#   ## тема | синоним   - о чём история (имя подставляется в просьбу пользователя)
#   - история           - одна строка, не длиннее ~180 символов (иначе не влезет в контекст 256)

## biters | biter | monsters
- one night biters came to my factory. my turrets shot them all, and in the morning i built a bigger wall.
- a small biter ate my belt. i was angry, but then i built laser turrets, and it never came back.
- once i made too much pollution. a big group of biters came, and i had to run to my car. now i use efficiency modules.

## trains | train
- i built my first train and sent it to get iron ore. it came back full, and i was so happy i rode it all day.
- once i forgot a rail signal. two trains crashed, and it was a big mess. now i always check my signals.
- one day i stood on the rails to look at my factory. a train came and hit me. i will never do that again!

## rocket | the rocket | rockets
- after many days of work, my rocket silo was ready. i pressed the button, and the rocket flew into the sky. i cried a little.
- my first rocket needed so many blue circuits. when it finally went up, all my robots danced around the silo.

## the crash | my ship | crash
- my ship fell from the sky and crashed on nauvis. i had only a pistol and a few iron plates. so i found some ore and built my first furnace.
- when i woke up after the crash, the grass was green and the biters were far away. i looked at the rocks and said, i will build a factory here.

## robots | robot
- i made my first construction robot. it flew out, built a whole wall for me and came back to charge. i gave it a name, bob.
- one day my robots ran out of power and sat still in the air. i built more solar panels, and they flew again.

## the factory | factory | my factory
- my factory started with one furnace. then there were ten, then a hundred. now it is so big that i drive a train to cross it.
- one morning all my machines stopped. i found out that one little belt was broken. i fixed it, and the factory hummed again.

## spaghetti | messy belts
- my first factory was all spaghetti. belts went left and right and under each other. it worked, but nobody understood how.
- i tried to clean my spaghetti belts, but i made even more spaghetti. now i just call it art.

## vulcanus | the hot planet
- i landed on vulcanus and saw rivers of lava. a huge demolisher crawled by, so i built my foundry far away from it.
- on vulcanus i mined tungsten for the first time. with it i made turbo belts, and they were so fast that i laughed.

## fulgora | the lightning planet
- on fulgora the night came with a storm. lightning hit my lightning rods again and again, and my accumulators filled up with power.
- i found an old city on fulgora full of scrap. my recyclers turned the junk into gears and circuits. it was like finding treasure.

## gleba | the swamp planet
- on gleba i grew yumako fruit. but i waited too long, and all my fruit turned into spoilage. now i am always in a hurry there.
- on gleba a big stomper walked into my farm. my rocket turrets stopped it just in time.

## aquilo | the ice planet
- aquilo was so cold that my pipes froze. i built heating towers everywhere, and slowly the factory warmed up.
- i sailed my platform to aquilo and saw the ocean of ammonia. it was beautiful and very cold.

## space | space platform | my space platform
- i built my first space platform. it flew through the stars, and my guns shot the asteroids that came too close.
- one day a big asteroid hit my platform and broke a thruster. i sent robots to fix it, and we flew on.

## the shattered planet | the edge of space
- my platform flew for a long time to the edge of the solar system. there i saw the shattered planet, broken into many pieces.

## power | electricity
- once i built too many machines and not enough steam engines. all the lights got dim and the machines slowed down. i built ten more engines.
- i set up solar panels and accumulators. at night the factory was quiet, but it never stopped.

## fish
- when i was hurt after a biter attack, i caught a fish from the lake and ate it. i felt much better.

## green circuits | circuits
- i built a whole building only for green circuits. copper cables went in, circuits came out, and it looked like a beautiful machine.

## oil
- my first oil refinery made too much heavy oil and stopped. i learned to crack it into light oil, and everything worked again.

## nauvis | home
- after visiting all the planets, i came back to nauvis. the grass was green, the trains were running, and i felt at home.
